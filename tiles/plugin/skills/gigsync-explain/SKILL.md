# Explain a GigSync agreement

1. Restate each term in one short sentence in the requested language: who pays whom, how much, for what unit (hour,
   visit, month, piece), on which days, at what hours, with which weekly off and paid-leave days, and the notice
   period.
2. Convert amounts from paise to rupees (100 paise = ₹1). Show monthly totals only when the rate unit allows it.
3. If two versions are shared, list what changed between them (an agreement with a new `terms_hash` replaces the old
   one; it never edits it).
4. Do not advise whether to accept. Say what the terms are and what is missing.
5. Copy placeholders such as `PHONE_1` exactly; never guess real values.
