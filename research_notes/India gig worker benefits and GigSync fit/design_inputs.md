Compiled 27 Oct 2026 from the six research notes in this folder and `reports/India gig worker benefits and GigSync fit.md`. Local files only. ## 1. The migrant's journey (Landing flow as a state machine)

**Who arrives.** 50–51% of two-wheeler delivery drivers are migrants (24% intra-state, 26% inter-state); avg age 28, <1% women, ~13-month tenure, net ₹18,761/mo after 32% costs on a 62-h week (IDinsight, n=2,547, Mar 2025, https://www.idinsight.org/article/the-changing-landscape-of-work-insights-into-indias-delivery-platform-gig-workforce/). Bengaluru's gig migrants: 66% north Karnataka, 20% local, ~14% other states **[U: ISEC PB-73 Dec 2025, snippet only]** (https://www.isec.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/PB-73-Governance-of-Gig-Platform-Economy.pdf). 40% of gig workers earn <₹15,000/mo (Economic Survey, 29 Jan 2026). Consequence: Landing recurs every ~13 months; intra-state movers (same bank/ration state and language) and inter-state movers need different checklists.

## 10. Gaps to treat as assumptions to test

1. **Time to first payout** and current onboarding fee/deposit by city for Zomato, Swiggy, Rapido, Zepto, UC: no official source. Assume ₹2,500–3,500 upfront and 1–2 weeks; measure in pilot.
2. **Days to open a bank account / update Aadhaar / get a SIM** for a newly arrived worker, and which step most often blocks platform activation: no data. SIM rules 2025–26 not researched.
3. **How newcomers find peers** (WhatsApp groups, rider stands, dhabas): no study; no platform referral amounts for partners. The union "connect me to workers from my region" feature has no evidence base.
4. **Per-bed rent paid by gig workers** in any metro: only one anecdote and formal-PG data. Nia's ₹1,500 is the only priced product.
5. **Remittance channels and cost** post-2019 for gig migrants, and whether recipients are banked: unknown.
6. **Whether any institution accepts a worker-held, counter-signed record as evidence** (Labourline, IDRC, Board, NBFC): no precedent found either way; also the minimum evidence accepted from a unilateral receipt.
7. **Whether workers will pay** for non-job services (only benchmark ₹25/day Namma Yatri) and whether a household employer will install an app and sign.
8. **Karnataka Domestic Workers Bill** passage and whether a phone-signed agreement counts as the written agreement; whether the state's model format will differ from the draft fields.
9. **Section 114 rate/start-date notification**, first scheme notification, PM-JAY card issuance to gig workers, Telangana commencement, Karnataka HC outcome after 14 Aug 2026: all unresolved as of 27 Oct 2026; check egazette before telling any worker they are "covered".
10. **e-Shram worker-facing API / downloadable history**, and whether Karnataka/Telangana rules give workers their own transaction data in any format: not determined.
11. **On-device ASR performance** (latency, RAM) for Sarvam Edge or IndicConformer INT8 on a sub-₹10,000 / 4 GB Android, and WebView performance: no benchmark.
12. **Union capacity and status of named orgs**: TGPWU >10,000 is self-reported; GIPSWU, DWRU, SEWA membership and 2025–26 activity not found; no partner MoU; Fairwork 2025 not retrieved; Tikaana, KarmaLife, Siply, Bimaplan, Qjobs, Gigforce have no 2025–26 coverage.
13. **Rest-point locations**: no public list or API; counts are company claims.
14. **Gig-specific onboarding scams** and fleet-owner ("seth") cuts: not researched; absence of reporting is not absence of practice.
15. **Legal status of person-to-person UPI** outside payment-aggregator rules: counsel not consulted (ADR 0011).
