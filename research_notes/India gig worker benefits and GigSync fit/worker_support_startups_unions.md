# India: organisations that serve gig / blue-collar workers directly (landscape as of October 2026)

Scope note: India only. "Status as of Oct 2026" means the most recent dated source I could find; where the latest source is 2023–2024 I say so and mark the current status as unverified. Every row cites a URL. Figures that come only from a company's own site, a government press release or a secondary data aggregator (CB Insights, Caplight, Clay) are marked as such. I ran ~40 search/fetch calls; several named organisations (Kaam.com, Hunar, Zippee, GigWA, SEWA's platform work, DWRU, PAIGAM report text, a Kerala gig-worker co-op, a Rajasthan platform co-op) returned nothing usable and are listed as gaps rather than guessed at.

## Key question 1: Who serves the worker, what do they offer, in which languages, at what price, at what scale, with what funding and status?

### Takeaway

The best-funded "worker-serving" companies are B2B at heart: jobs marketplaces sell to employers (Apna, WorkIndia, Vahan, Betterplace, Awign), fintechs distribute through employers (Refyne, Onsurity, Plum), and most have drifted upmarket or consolidated (Awign to Mynavi, Taskmo to Quess, GigIndia to PhonePe; Apna now lists more white-collar than blue-collar jobs). The only direct-to-worker, worker-governed models with real scale are the open-mobility / cooperative ride-hailing apps (Namma Yatri ~4 lakh drivers in 2024, Yatri Sathi 1.15 lakh, Bharat Taxi 4.3 lakh claimed in Mar 2026), and those are vertical (drivers only). Unions are vocal and nationally coordinated (IFAT, TGPWU, GIPSWU) but small in paid membership and tool-poor.

### Inferences

- Capital has consolidated around employer-paid models; the three "serve-the-worker" ideas that attracted the most money (Apna, Betterplace, Refyne) all monetise the employer and have each either narrowed losses by cutting growth or drifted to higher-income users. A worker-first app cannot copy their funding path.
- The worker-owned/government models prove drivers will switch for zero commission, but every one of them is a ride-hailing vertical. There is no cross-sector equivalent (delivery, domestic work, construction, warehouse).
- Unions have proven national coordination (Dec 2025–Feb 2026 strike wave) but publish no membership tooling, dues platform or member app; they rely on WhatsApp and press. They are natural distribution partners rather than competitors.

### Gaps

- Kaam.com, Hunar, Zippee, GigWA, SEWA platform initiatives, DWRU, PAIGAM report, Kerala gig co-op, Rajasthan platform co-ops: no usable sources found.
- Current (2025–26) status for Qjobs, Gigforce, KarmaLife, Bimaplan, Siply, Mana Yatri.
- Fairwork India 2025 edition: existence implied by Dec 2025 press but not retrieved.
- Apna's current blue-collar vs white-collar user split and 2026 MAU: not disclosed.

## Key question 2: Which of these help a worker who moves to a new city (work, place to stay, peer network, documents, bank/UPI, SIM, ID)?

### Takeaway

Almost nobody. Jobs apps cover "find work" (and only for listed, employer-verified roles); the ride co-ops onboard drivers in a handful of cities; housing is served by one hostel in Surat and no app; documents/ID/bank/SIM are not served by any startup found, and the government's e-Shram ID is the only portable identity.

### Cited Findings

- **Find work:** Apna (14 lakh postings in 2025) — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/business/ai-adoption-in-hiring-4-years-of-recruiter-calling-time-saved-3839483); Vahan places ~40,000/month into delivery via WhatsApp — [Venture Intelligence](https://news.ventureintelligence.com/ma/blue-collar-workers-hiring-platform-vahan.ai-raises-funding-from-temasek’s-lemmatree;-acquires-upski); Lokal carries local job classifieds in 6 languages — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2023/04/lokal-raises-rs-120-cr-creator-content-hyperlocal-bengaluru-startup); Yatri Sathi/Namma Yatri/Bharat Taxi onboard drivers only in their live cities (lists above).
- **Place to stay:** Fewer than 42,000 rental units built under the national ARHC scheme as of April 2025; urban rental housing policy a draft since 2015 — [IDR](https://idronline.org/article/urban/reimagining-urban-housing-for-migrant-workers/). Aajeevika Bureau: schemes focus on ownership, migrants without domicile documents are excluded — [The Wire](https://thewire.in/economy/housing-for-all-migrant-workers). Only concrete worker-hostel example found: Republic Hostel & Mess, Sayan, Surat (Oct 2023) — [The Migration Story](https://themigrationstory.com/?p=490).
- **Peer network:** Unions (TGPWU >10,000 registered) — [TGPWU](https://tgpwu.org/?p=2); Apna "communities" (no 2025 usage data found). No dedicated rider-community app found.
- **Documents / ID:** e-Shram gives an Aadhaar-linked unique ID for benefit portability across platforms — [IANS](https://ianslive.in/new-labour-codes-give-gig-platform-workers-portable-social-security-benefits--20251209160443); Karnataka and Telangana Acts issue a state unique ID on registration — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/bills_states/telangana/2025/Brief_TG_Gig_Workers_Bill_2025.pdf). Verification of a worker's background in India takes 15–21 days on average (DESH Stack study) — [Sattva](https://www.sattva.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Skilling-and-Employment-Potential-for-Gig-Workers-through-DESH-Stack.pdf).
- **Bank/UPI, SIM:** No organisation in the five groups offers help with these; Jar and Siply assume an existing UPI-linked account — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=351573), [CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/2-5-year-old-micro-savings-fintech-siply-turns-profitable-records-an-8x-growth-in-revenue-in-just-six-months/).
- **Insurance that follows the worker:** Platform cover (Zomato/Swiggy) is tied to activity on that platform; unions say it is not cashless — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/insurance-promises-vs-ground-reality-gig-workers-push-back-on-zomato-swiggy-claims). The Code on Social Security is explicitly designed so "workers retain benefits even when switching between platforms" — [DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2026/india-mandates-aggregator-onboarding-to-eshram-portal-to-expand-social-security-coverage).

### Inferences

- A "landing in a new city" bundle (work + room + ID/e-Shram registration + bank/SIM + a known peer) has no incumbent; the pieces that exist are fragmented across a jobs app, a union WhatsApp group, a government portal and (rarely) an NGO hostel.
- The e-Shram deadline pressure on aggregators (Jun 2026) makes a third-party app that helps a worker register, keep and show their e-Shram/state ID valuable to both worker and platform.

### Gaps

- No survey found that quantifies what share of gig workers are inter-state migrants or how they find their first job/room in a new city. Aajeevika's reports cover migrant labour broadly, not app-based gig work.
- No data on union membership portability across cities.

## Key question 3: Who holds or builds a portable work history / record / credential for the worker, and who controls it?

### Takeaway

No startup found offers a worker-controlled, cross-platform work record. Records are held by platforms (ratings, trip counts), by employers/BGV vendors (Betterplace, AuthBridge), or by government (e-Shram ID and state welfare-board IDs). Namma Yatri's open-data stance is the closest to worker-visible data, but it is still platform-held.

### Cited Findings

- e-Shram: Aadhaar-linked unique ID; aggregators must share worker data electronically with government within 45 days of the Rules (8 May 2026) — [DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2026/india-mandates-aggregator-onboarding-to-eshram-portal-to-expand-social-security-coverage). Controlled by the Labour Ministry.
- State Acts (Karnataka 2025, Telangana draft/2026) register workers and issue IDs via a welfare board; Telangana's requires algorithmic transparency — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/bills_states/telangana/2025/Brief_TG_Gig_Workers_Bill_2025.pdf), [Mondaq](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/india/employee-rights-labour-relations/1713454/karnataka-platform-based-gig-workers-social-security-and-welfare-act-2025).
- DESH Stack study (Sattva, 2022): gig workers "lose their standing" when moving platforms; calls for digitally verifiable credentials — [Sattva PDF](https://www.sattva.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Skilling-and-Employment-Potential-for-Gig-Workers-through-DESH-Stack.pdf).
- Betterplace positions itself as the employer-side onboarding/BGV/workforce record (Series D 2025) — [BetterPlace](https://www.betterplace.co.in/company); AuthBridge frames work-history tracking as a verification-industry need — [AuthBridge](https://authbridge.com/?p=94337). Both are employer-controlled.
- KaushalID: a FOSS Hack 2026 project for a worker-held skill/work-history profile with employer validation — [FOSS United](https://fossunited.org/hack/fosshack26/p/frpfns3bu1) (hackathon prototype, not a product).
- Namma Yatri publishes open data metrics and is open-source — [Antler](https://www.antler.co/press-releases/namma-yatri-raises-11-million-to-revolutionize-mobility-and-build-seamless-public-transportation).
- Apna's 2025 data shows AI interviews becoming the screening record (73 lakh sessions) — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/business/ai-adoption-in-hiring-4-years-of-recruiter-calling-time-saved-3839483) — a record Apna, not the worker, controls.

### Inferences

- The regulatory data flow now runs platform → government (e-Shram). Nothing returns it to the worker in a form they can carry to the next employer. A worker-held, offline-capable record that mirrors e-Shram/state IDs plus platform ratings is white space.

### Gaps

- Could not find any Indian startup shipping verifiable-credential (VC/DID) wallets for blue-collar workers in production.
- Whether e-Shram exposes a worker-facing API or downloadable history: not determined.

## Key question 4: What do workers themselves report they need most?

### Takeaway

Across Fairwork, Janpahal, CIS, IFAT and the Economic Survey, the consistent asks are: a pay floor (per hour/task incl. wait time), accident and health insurance that actually pays (cashless), fewer hours for the same income, safety (heat, roads, harassment especially for women), rest/water/toilet infrastructure, transparent algorithms, a grievance channel, and recognition as workers; housing and documents appear in migrant-worker research rather than gig surveys.

### Cited Findings

- Janpahal OSH report (1,000+ workers, 10 metros, Feb 2026): >50% work >49 hrs/week, ~25% >70 hrs; ~half have no accident or health insurance; >50% buy their own drinking water; heat affects almost all; demands = pay and social security, insurance that is obtainable, road safety, protection from violence/harassment, water/washrooms/rest stations, OSH coverage — [The Wire](https://m.thewire.in/article/economy/more-than-20-gig-workers-work-70-hours-per-week-report). Other coverage of the same study: 62% near-miss accidents, 27% accidents, 44% no accident insurance, 62% no health insurance, ~60% no safety training, 72% on platforms >1 year — [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/india/25-gig-workers-clock-over-70-hours-weekly-shocking-survey/tldr) (figures vary by outlet).
- Janpahal earlier survey (5,220 workers): 85% work 8+ hrs/day without social security; 65% of women feel unsafe — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/85-of-platform-based-gig-workers-put-in-8-hrs-daily-but-dont-get-basic-social-security-ngo-survey/1992201).
- Economic Survey 2025-26: ~40% of gig workers earn <₹15,000/month; recommends minimum per-hour/per-task earnings incl. wait time, algorithm transparency, portable social security — [Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/business-news/latest-updates/40-of-gig-workers-earn-under-15-000-a-month-economic-survey-pitches-pay-floors-algorithm-checks/article-188520).
- Fairwork India 2024: 440 interviews; platforms fail most on fair pay (minimum wage after costs), contracts, due process and collective representation; Ola/Porter/Uber scored 0/10 — [Fairwork 2024 PDF](https://production.ruralindiaonline.org/uploads/Fairwork_India_Ratings_2024_3978a6c53b.pdf).
- IFAT 2023 driver survey: 74% call incentive systems unfair; 65% earn less than pre-2020 — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/sarathi-hi-malik-how-bharat-taxi-debut-aims-to-disrupt-ride-hailing-economics-514865-2026-02-05).
- Union strike demands (Dec 2025–Feb 2026): employee status, statutory minimum wage, km-based payouts after fuel hike, right to associate, grievance mechanism, central gig law — [Millennium Post](https://www.millenniumpost.in/delhi/gig-workers-union-announces-diwali-digital-strike-demand-fair-wages-rights-584752), [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/app-based-gig-workers-union-calls-for-5-hour-nationwide-shutdown/), [FPJ](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/gig-platform-service-workers-union-announces-nationwide-app-switch-off-protest-on-january-26).
- Insurance reality: Telangana union leaders say platform cover has no cashless option and workers pay out of pocket — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/insurance-promises-vs-ground-reality-gig-workers-push-back-on-zomato-swiggy-claims); documented claim denial — [Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/2024/swiggy-health-insurance-quotas/).
- CIS Labour Futures (800 workers): covered earnings, hours, work-related costs, platform management — [ISOC Foundation](https://www.isocfoundation.org/project/labour-futures-intersectional-policy-making-for-the-platform-economy) (detailed findings not retrieved).
- Migrant workers (Aajeevika): rental housing and documentation/domicile are the binding constraints — [The Wire](https://thewire.in/economy/housing-for-all-migrant-workers), [IDR](https://idronline.org/article/urban/reimagining-urban-housing-for-migrant-workers/).
- Delivery partners find financing hard as NBFCs stay cautious (2025) — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/07/ev-financing-challenge-haunting-indias-electric-vehicle-transition).

### Inferences

- The needs that an app (vs a law) can actually address: knowing one's real hourly earnings after costs, finding cashless care/claims help, locating rest/water points, a safe channel for harassment reporting, and a way to prove hours/incidents for claims and grievances. Pay floors and employee status need legislation, which unions are already pursuing.

### Gaps

- No PAIGAM, ILO-India 2025 or Fairwork India 2025 primary text located; worker-need rankings above come from press coverage of surveys, not the reports.
- No survey found that asks gig workers specifically about moving cities, housing, or document loss.

## Key question 5: Where is the obvious white space?

### Takeaway

Five gaps recur across the evidence: (1) a worker-held portable record (hours, incidents, ratings, e-Shram/state IDs); (2) a new-city landing bundle; (3) cross-sector worker-first infrastructure outside ride-hailing; (4) tooling for unions (membership, dues, grievance logging, strike coordination beyond WhatsApp); (5) help actually using the insurance and welfare-board money that now exists on paper.

### Cited Findings

- Every worker-owned/state platform found is ride-hailing (Namma Yatri, Mana Yatri, Yatri Sathi, Kerala Savaari, Bharat Taxi) — see tables above; no delivery, domestic-work or construction equivalent surfaced ([Antler](https://www.antler.co/press-releases/namma-yatri-raises-11-million-to-revolutionize-mobility-and-build-seamless-public-transportation), [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/sarathi-hi-malik-how-bharat-taxi-debut-aims-to-disrupt-ride-hailing-economics-514865-2026-02-05)).
- Government money is now mandated (1–2% of aggregator turnover; Karnataka 1–5% per transaction) but rules for collection and portability are described as "skeletal" — [DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2026/india-mandates-aggregator-onboarding-to-eshram-portal-to-expand-social-security-coverage), [Mondaq](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/india/employee-rights-labour-relations/1713454/karnataka-platform-based-gig-workers-social-security-and-welfare-act-2025).
- Fintech for gig workers is employer-gated (Refyne, Onsurity, Plum) or dormant/unverified (KarmaLife, Bimaplan, Siply) — rows above.
- Jobs apps monetise employers and are moving upmarket (Apna >50% white-collar) — [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/business/apna-uses-ai-to-match-blue-collar-workers-aims-rs150cr-revenue/tldr).
- Unions coordinate nationally but publish only PDFs and press releases; membership self-reported at ~10k registered for the largest state union — [TGPWU](https://tgpwu.org/?p=2).
- Verification takes 15–21 days; workers lose standing when switching platforms — [Sattva](https://www.sattva.co.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Skilling-and-Employment-Potential-for-Gig-Workers-through-DESH-Stack.pdf).

### Inferences

- Competitors for a worker-first app are, in practice, WhatsApp groups and the platforms' own apps; partners are unions (distribution and trust), the Juspay/ONDC open-mobility stack (interoperability), and state welfare boards (registration).
- Risk to the thesis: Bharat Taxi and state boards could bundle insurance, loans and ID themselves for drivers, shrinking the vertical where co-ops already exist; the defensible space is cross-sector and worker-held data, which no government body is building.

### Gaps

- No market-size estimate for "worker-held record" or "new-city landing" services exists in the sources found.
- No evidence either way on whether workers would pay (even ₹25/day, the Namma Yatri benchmark) for non-job services.
