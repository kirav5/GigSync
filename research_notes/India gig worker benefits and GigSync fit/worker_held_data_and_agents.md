# Worker-held work records, data rights and worker-side agents (India and global), as of 27 Oct 2026

Scope note: this covers everyone found building in or near "the worker's own agent on the worker's own phone that agrees each job, releases contact after both sign, and keeps a signed, selectively-shareable record of jobs and payments". Research method: web search + fetch of project sites, court/press coverage and legal commentary. Several primary pages (driversseat.co, fair.work principles, gigbox.media.mit.edu, EUR-Lex) returned 404/DNS errors or empty content on fetch; where that happened the finding is marked **unverified** or moved to Gaps. All dates are as reported by the cited source.

## KQ1. Global projects: who built worker-held data tools, what happened to them

### Takeaway

Every global worker-data tool found is either dead, dormant, absorbed into a university research lab, or has pivoted from "worker-held record" to "litigation/collective data access". The one durable model is Worker Info Exchange (UK): it does not give workers a ledger; it files GDPR subject-access and portability requests on their behalf and litigates. No project was found that lets a worker keep a signed job-by-job record that institutions accept.

EU Platform Work Directive (EU) 2024/2831:
- Published in the Official Journal 11 Nov 2024; transposition deadline 2 Dec 2026 — [Learnsignal summary](https://www.learnsignal.com/blog/platform-workers-directive-gig-economy-guide/); [Mason Hayes & Curran](https://www.mhc.ie/latest/insights/the-platform-work-directive).
- Content (per law-firm summaries): platforms must inform workers about automated monitoring and decision-making systems; significant decisions (e.g. account restriction/suspension) subject to human review; platforms barred from processing certain data (emotional/psychological state, private conversations, data collected while not working); platforms must give national authorities data on headcount, status, earnings and hours — [Garrigues](https://bloglaboral.garrigues.com/en/riders-law-and-platform-work-directive-challenges-ahead); [CXC Global](https://www.cxcglobal.com/blog/cxc-comply/eu-directive-on-platform-workers/).

### Inferences

- The pattern across Driver's Seat, Gigbox, Shipt Calculator and Rodeo: the technology (earnings tracking, OCR of pay screenshots, cross-platform aggregation) was never the bottleneck. Projects died or stalled on (a) platforms blocking data access (Deliveroo v Rodeo), (b) community-building and retention (Gigbox post-mortem, Driver's Seat usability decline), and (c) grant dependence (Mozilla funded three of them).
- What survives is institutional leverage over the platform's data (WIE's GDPR requests and litigation), not the worker's own ledger. The Amsterdam ruling that PDFs do not count as machine-readable is directly relevant to a "signed record" design: a court has already said what format a usable record must take.
- The EU directive creates a right-to-explanation and human-review regime but, per the summaries found, does not create a worker-held portable record; GDPR Art. 20 portability remains the vehicle, and WIE shows it takes litigation to make it bite.

- Could not confirm Driver's Seat Cooperative's legal status (dissolved vs dormant) or the WAO transition date; the co-op's site is down.

- Swash and Pool status 2025–26: no sources found.

## KQ2. India: state, platform, union and research efforts on portable worker records and data rights

### Takeaway

India has no worker-held, platform-independent work record in production. The state answer is registration (e-Shram ~11.5 lakh platform workers by Aug 2026; Rajasthan/Karnataka/Telangana welfare-board IDs) plus Karnataka's Section 13 right to ask how algorithms work; none of these gives the worker an exportable, signed job-by-job record, and the DPDP Act 2023 has no portability right. Unions (IFAT, TGPWU) have explicitly demanded download/transfer rights since at least 2023, which is the clearest demand signal found.

- Multiple analyses state the DPDP Act has no data-portability right (rights are access, correction, erasure, grievance, nomination); DPDP Rules 2025 notified 13 Nov 2025 with staged commencement — [Jindal Policy Research Lab](https://jgu.edu.in/jsgp/jindal-policy-research-lab/digital-personal-data-protection-act-2023/); [Legiscope DPDP vs GDPR](https://www.legiscope.com/blog/dpdp-act-vs-gdpr.html); [Bird & Bird](https://twobirds.com/en/insights/2023/global/decrypting-indias-new-data-protection-law-key-insights-and-lessons-learned).

- BetterPlace (Bengaluru): background verification for blue-collar hires combining Aadhaar, digital footprint, checks and crowdsourcing; 2017 framing of "building their credentials" and linking to credit/insurance; sources are 2016–17 — [Business Today 2017](https://www.businesstoday.in/magazine/features/story/the-trust-platform-84685-2017-08-04); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/flash-feed/betterplace-funding-unitus-seed-fund).
- Apna: Sep 2025 launched "Apna Safety" verifying recruiters, not workers; a third-party note says most blue-collar apps "don't verify workers" — [Business Today Sep 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/apnaco-launches-ai-powered-tool-apna-safety-to-curb-job-scams-in-india-during-festive-hiring-495735-2025-09-25); [Notion essay](https://bigansh.notion.site/The-hard-to-crack-blue-collar-job-industry-835fd229c43141d7b40db90ba0f88531).

### Inferences

- Every Indian "record" is employer/platform-submitted and state-held (e-Shram, welfare boards), i.e. the opposite of worker-held. The Karnataka S.13 right is a right to an explanation on request, with no format, no auditor and no collective access, so it does not produce a record a union could use.
- Union demand for download/transfer rights exists and is documented (TGPWU 2023), which is evidence of demand for the "selectively show to a union" feature. However the demand is framed as access to the _platform's_ data, not a worker-generated ledger; a GigSync-style record would need to be recognised as evidence to matter.
- Absence of a DPDP portability right means an Indian worker cannot compel a platform to hand over machine-readable data the way the Amsterdam court compelled Uber. A worker-held record captured at the moment of agreement is therefore the only route to a machine-readable record in India today.

### Gaps

- Rajasthan 2025–26 implementation (rules notified? cess collected? workers registered?) — no sources found.
- Whether Karnataka or Telangana Rules require platforms to give workers their own transaction data in any format — not in sources retrieved.
- Whether Zomato/Swiggy/Urban Company partner apps export statements — unverified.
- Any Indian court decision relying on a worker's self-kept record — none found.
- IT for Change / CIS post-2022 outputs on worker data rights — not retrieved.

## KQ4. Income proof for lenders and demand for a cross-platform ledger

### Takeaway

Gig workers prove income with platform app summaries plus bank statements; NBFCs and digital lenders accept these, PSU banks less reliably (one non-authoritative source). The two structural efforts to replace this are platform-linked (KarmaLife: platform pays a subscription, payout account linked) and regulator-linked (RBIH + Vivifi pilot, late 2024, consent-based pull of gig-platform activity plus bureau data, to be folded into ULI); neither is worker-held, and no pilot results were published. CGAP notes platform churn erodes the value of single-platform data, which is the demand argument for a cross-platform ledger, but no lender was found that accepts a worker-held or platform-independent record.

### Cited Findings

- KarmaLife (Bengaluru, founded 2020): earned-wage access, credit lines, loans; platforms pay a subscription so the worker's KarmaLife account links to the platform payout account; underwriting on behaviour "rather than paperwork"; credit lines and insurance "in pilot mode" at time of writing — [BFA Global](https://bfaglobal.com/?p=223036); [YourStory 2022](https://yourstory.com/2022/07/httpsyourstorycom202207fintech-startup-karmalife-provides-financial-services-to-gig-workers). No source on NBFC licence or AA use.
- CGAP "Putting Gig Data To Work": churn is "more or less baked into the design of platform work, eroding the value of that work data for assessing creditworthiness"; names KarmaLife a pioneer — [CGAP PDF](https://www.cgap.org/sites/default/files/publications/Putting%20Gig%20Data%20To%20Work.pdf).
- RBI Innovation Hub pilot with Vivifi Finance and three gig platforms (reported Dec 2024): worker registers on lender app, consents to retrieval of gig-platform activity and bureau data; plan to integrate with Unified Lending Interface after pilot; no outcomes published — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/rbi-innovation-hub-pilots-unsecured-loan-offerings-for-gig-workers); [RBIH Design Shaala](https://designshaala.rbihub.in/?p=2245); [SightsInPlus](https://www.sightsinplus.com/news/partnership/rbi-innovation-hub-initiates-digital-loan-pilot-for-gig-economy).
- Account Aggregator: ~1,000 entities, 30 crore consents, Rs 17,000 crore monthly disbursements (Sahamati CEO interview); AA moves bank/financial data under consent but cannot read or store it; loan underwriting is a live use case for new-to-credit borrowers; no gig-specific AA pilot found — [Outlook Business interview](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/amp/story/interviews/targeted-incentives-could-bolster-account-aggregator-to-upi-levels-sahamati-ceo-2); [Sahamati](https://sahamati.org.in/?p=3932); [RBIH assisted-AA workshop](https://sahamati.org.in/events/sahamati-at-rbihs-assisted-account-aggregator-workshop/).
- Lender marketing: Credit Saison says AA lets borrowers share bank statements digitally and that merchant QR/UPI transaction history can serve as income proof for UPI-paid gig workers — [Credit Saison blog](https://creditsaison.in/blog/business-loans-for-gig-workers-no-salary-slip).
- A lending blog claims Swiggy/Zomato in-app earnings summaries are accepted by "most NBFCs and digital lenders" alongside six months of bank statements but "PSU banks don't uniformly accept these" — **unverified**, single source — [Credit Compass](https://creditcompass-black.vercel.app/blog/personal-loan-gig-workers-india-zomato-swiggy-uber-2026).
- Urban Company channelled Rs 11.27 crore in personal/kit loans to partners via third-party NBFCs in H1 CY2023, i.e. platform-intermediated credit — [UC blog](https://www.urbancompany.com/blog/monthly-earnings-for-urban-company-partners-grow-by-over-17-uc-earnings-index-h1-cy-2023).
- Zomato CEO: delivery partners working 8+ hrs/day averaged ~Rs 28,000/month in 2024; Rs 102/hr in 2025 (all logged-in time); TGPWU disputed, computing ~Rs 81/hr — [Business Today Feb 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/rs-28000-average-monthly-earnings-deepinder-goyal-shares-how-zomato-revolutionised-gig-workers-earnings-and-benefits-463251-2025-02-03); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/jobs-flexibility-10-pay-hike-deepinder-goyal-defends-gig-model-amid-workers-strike).

### Inferences

- Lenders today trust two sources: the platform (via API/subscription link) and the bank (via AA/statements). A worker-held ledger is a third source with no acceptance precedent; for it to be useful to a lender it would need either (a) counterparty signatures (customer and worker) so it is not self-attested, or (b) reconciliation against UPI/bank credits via AA. Off-platform domestic work paid in cash or UPI is exactly where (b) fails and (a) is the only option.
- The RBIH/ULI direction suggests the regulator's preferred path is platform-to-lender consented data flows, which excludes off-platform work. That is the gap a GigSync-style record would fill, but there is no evidence yet that any lender would accept it.

### Gaps

- No lender (bank or NBFC) found that states it accepts a platform-independent or worker-generated record.
- RBIH/Vivifi pilot results (approval, default, volumes): none published as found.
- KarmaLife's current scale, licence and whether it uses AA: not found.
- No survey evidence of worker demand for a cross-platform ledger; demand is inferred from union data-access demands (KQ2) and CGAP's churn argument.

## KQ5. Domestic / home-service work: written agreements, registration, placement agencies, digital contract tools

### Takeaway

No digital contract or record tool for household employment in India was found. The closest legal driver is Karnataka's draft Domestic Workers (Social Security and Welfare) Bill 2025 (released 15 Oct 2025), which would make a written agreement on a state-notified model format mandatory and require employer, worker and any placement agency or digital platform to register on a government portal within 30 days; it had not been confirmed as passed by Oct 2026. Existing boards (Kerala since 2018, Tamil Nadu's Domestic Workers Welfare Board, Delhi's 2014 placement-agency order) register workers or agencies but do not record jobs or agreements.

### Cited Findings

- Karnataka draft bill (15 Oct 2025, 30-day objection window; expected in Belagavi winter session): no domestic worker to be engaged without a written agreement mirroring a model format the State will notify, covering name, nature of work, daily hours, wages, leave, welfare fee contribution, termination; registration on portal within 30 days after signing; worker gets a 3-year renewable certificate with unique ID; fines up to Rs 50,000 and 3–7 years' jail for specified violations; applies to placement agencies and app platforms — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/labour/karnataka-domestic-workers-bill-2025/); [Citizen Matters](https://citizenmatters.in/a-step-towards-dignity-karnatakas-draft-domestic-workers-bill-explained/); [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/karnataka/karnataka-unveils-draft-law-to-safeguard-rights-of-domestic-workers-makes-registration-mandatory); [ETV Bharat Nov 2025](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/state/domestic-workers-in-karnataka-will-soon-get-statutory-protection-enn25110404785); [draft PDF](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/2b7c10e8-7a1c-4468-b481-66c15aa1a0f0/resource/5a2ff1ff-4ed9-4796-a8c9-a287576b43c8/download/domestic-workers-draft-bill.pdf). Unions have concerns about the draft — [TNM explainer](https://www.thenewsminute.com/karnataka/explainer-karnataka-domestic-workers-draft-bill-and-why-unions-are-concerned).
- Supreme Court (Jan 2025) directed states to frame protective measures for domestic workers — [Shankar IAS summary](https://www.shankariasparliament.com/current-affairs/gs-ii/domestic-workers-in-india).
- Domestic Workers' Rights Union (Bengaluru): demanded mandatory registration and oversight of all placement agencies and digital platforms; condemned paid "background verification" marketing by platforms such as BookMyBai as stigmatising — [The Wire](https://m.thewire.in/article/labour/fear-sold-as-safety-domestic-workers-groups-slam-hiring-platforms-seek-regulation).
- Delhi Private Placement Agencies (Regulation) Order 2014 (under Delhi HC direction in Bachpan Bachao Andolan): regulates agencies supplying domestic workers; a Delhi draft policy proposes mandatory registration, written contracts and social security (date/status unknown) — [WIEGO: Delhi order](https://www.wiego.org/resources/delhi-private-placement-agency-regulation-order-2014); [Shankar IAS](https://www.shankariasparliament.com/current-affairs/gs-ii/gs-ii-social-justice/domestic-workers-in-india). WIEGO argues agency registration alone "is not a real solution" because workers cross state lines — [WIEGO legal issues paper](https://www.wiego.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Domestic-Workers-Laws-and-Legal-Issues-India.pdf).
- Labour Ministry advised states to regulate private placement agencies and expanded e-Shram-linked benefits for domestic workers — [KSK newsletter](https://ksandk.com/newsletter/e-shram-domestic-workers-benefits-expanded-by-labour-ministry/).
- Kerala: Unorganized Workers Social Security Board formed 2016, registering domestic workers since 2018; receives no employer contribution — [IDWF brief](https://idwfed.org/?p=31822).
- Tamil Nadu: Domestic Workers Welfare Board is one of 18 unorganised-worker boards (CAG appendix); no enrolment figures found; TN domestic workers' unions presented demands to the government — [CAG appendix PDF](https://cag.gov.in/uploads/download_audit_report/2024/12-Appendices-066826f747fcdf0.19064004.pdf); [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/tn-domestic-workers-place-key-demands-to-govt-837306); [WIEGO TN](https://www.wiego.org/research-library-publications/domestic-workers-and-social-protection-tamil-nadu).
- SEWA Delhi organises domestic-worker unions for wages, conditions and paid leave; no SEWA model contract found in search — [Shankar IAS](https://www.shankariasparliament.com/current-affairs/gs-ii/domestic-workers-in-india).
- CIS 2021 fieldwork with DWRU: platforms "rely on and amplify unequal structures of power"; digital intermediation shows "high-growth, but also high-attrition" — [CIS 2021](https://editors.cis-india.org/raw/platforms-power-and-politics-perspectives-from-domestic-and-care-work-in-india).

### Inferences

- If Karnataka's bill passes in its draft form, every household employment in the state would legally require a written agreement on a notified template plus portal registration within 30 days. That is the single most concrete regulatory hook found for a phone-based agreement-and-record tool, and it would apply to platforms and agencies as well as households.
- Current boards record _workers_, not _jobs_; a job-level signed record is additive to, not competing with, board registration, and could be the evidence a board needs for welfare-fee or dispute claims.
- DWRU's hostility to platform-run "background verification" implies a worker-side tool must avoid becoming another verification/surveillance layer on the worker; the record should be of the agreement and payment, held by the worker.

### Gaps

- Karnataka Domestic Workers Bill status after Nov 2025 (tabled/passed/amended): no source found.
- SEWA or DWRU model contract text: not found.
- Kerala/TN domestic-worker registration counts 2025: not found.
- Any Indian or global digital contract/record tool for household employment: none found (search returned Qatar's state "Moawen" recruitment platform only, which is a government recruitment portal, not a worker-held record — [Qatar Tribune](https://www.qatar-tribune.com/article/253001/nation/mol-launches-phase-one-of-moawen-platform-for-domestic-worker-recruitment)).
