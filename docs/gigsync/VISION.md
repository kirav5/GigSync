# GigSync — Vision

> Status: **v1.1, 28 Oct 2026.** This page says what GigSync is for and in what order it gets built. The
> [HLD](HLD.md) says how, the [LLD](LLD.md) says exactly how, and [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) says when. The
> market evidence behind every claim here is in
> [the October 2026 landscape report](../../reports/India%20gig%20worker%20benefits%20and%20GigSync%20fit.md).

## 1. One sentence

**GigSync is a gig worker's own agent, on their own phone, for their whole working life:** it finds work in any
mode, agrees each job, keeps the worker's signed record of jobs and payments, and, when the worker moves to a new
city, lands them there: a verified cheap bed, workers from their own region, registration for the welfare they are
owed, and the documents they need.

GigSync never stands between the worker and the customer, the money, or the record. Partners and unions do what
needs a human or a building.

## 2. Who it is for

| Person                       | What they get                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The worker**               | An agent that works for them alone: finds and agrees jobs, keeps their record, lands them in a new city, protects them. Domestic work, beauty and home services first; delivery, rides and construction on the same core.                           |
| **The household / consumer** | A safe way to hire: a worker whose terms are written down, whose contact arrives only once both sign, and whose work is countersigned so disputes have a record. No install needed: a household can read, sign and pay from a WhatsApp or SMS link. |
| **The union or steward**     | A desk that receives only what a member chooses to send, issues flags members trust, runs the circles newcomers join, and helps members claim what the law now owes them.                                                                           |
| **The partner**              | A hostel, a helpline, a skilling body or a lender gets a worker who arrives with a verified record and a consent, not a cold lead.                                                                                                                  |

## 3. Five promises

Each promise is a part of the agent. Each says what GigSync does and what it deliberately leaves to others.

| Promise        | The agent does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | GigSync never                                                                                |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Work**       | Finds work in every mode the worker wants: one-off visits, part-time households, full-time placement, platform shifts. | **Record**     | Keeps a signed, hash-chained record of every agreement, visit and payment, on the phone, across every platform and every city. ## 5. Why in this order

The founders' vision is the full journey. The build order is the record first, then landing, then circles, then
the rest. Three reasons, each from the evidence rather than preference:
1. **The record is the only part of the journey nobody else is building.** Platforms keep their own records and
   cover lapses when the worker leaves. Government builds registries (e-Shram, state boards) that hold what
   platforms upload, not what the worker did off-platform. Every worker-data tool abroad died ([Driver's Seat,
   Gigbox, Rodeo](https://civictech.guide/projects/gigbox)). A countersigned, portable, worker-held record of
   off-platform work is white space, and it is what every later step consumes: the landing partner wants to know
   who is arriving, the union wants evidence, the lender wants income proof, the Board wants the day count.
2. **Landing without a record is a directory.** Beds, helplines and rest points exist already; what does not
   exist is a worker arriving with a verified history and a consent to share a slice of it. Partners are the
   right way to deliver housing and credit (Nia.one is real estate plus a staffing commission; a lender is a
   balance sheet), and a partner integration is only as good as what the worker can hand over.
3. **Circles need a steward, and the steward is the union.** Every global peer-community tool for gig workers
   failed on community-building, not code. Unions already have the WhatsApp groups and the trust;
   [TGPWU helped 62% of its members register](https://tgpwu.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/TGPWU_ImpactEvaluationReport_2023_Digital-1.pdf).
   GigSync gives the union the tooling and the governance, and does not try to be the community.

The two things dropped from the earlier plan, and why: the **ONDC relay** for rides and delivery enters a vertical
where [Namma Yatri, Yatri Sathi and Bharat Taxi](https://theprint.in/india/governance/amit-shah-launches-bharat-taxi-the-govt-backed-alternative-to-ola-uber-drivers-will-be-owners/2847000)
already hold the drivers and are bundling benefits; it returns only when a partner asks. **Bluesky at onboarding**
added an email and a CAPTCHA to a low-literacy flow for a public profile the board already provides; it becomes an
option for workers who want a public identity.

The **job board is kept**, because it is the demand side of direct household work and is already built
([PR #8](https://github.com/kirav5/GigSync/pull/8)). What changes is what it is not: GigSync does not rank
workers, does not take a cut, and does not try to out-liquidity Apna or Vahan. Discovery is by trade, distance
and availability; the agent, not the board, does the matching.

## 6. What GigSync is not

- **Not a platform.** No employer, agency or platform in the middle; a job is two agents and two signatures.
- **Not a bank.** UPI goes straight to the worker. No wallet, no escrow, no lending.
- **Not a landlord, a school or an insurer.** Partners are; GigSync hands over a worker and a consent.
- **Not a rating system.** No stars. A record says "worked together, countersigned, N times", which is a fact.
- **Not a database of workers.** The cloud holds keys, public cards, locked envelopes and a partner registry.
- **Not AI-dependent.** Works with no model at all; a small offline model only helps with speech and wording.
- **Not a WhatsApp bot.** WhatsApp and SMS are doors: they carry a notice and a link. The agent, the terms, the contact cards and the record never pass through Meta's servers ([ADR 0021](../adr/0021-whatsapp-as-a-door-never-the-agent.md)).
- **Not an escrow.** Payment by stage is countersigned receipts plus direct UPI at each stage; GigSync never holds a rupee ([ADR 0022](../adr/0022-payment-schedules-by-day-and-stage-no-escrow.md)).

## 7a. How it fits together

| Part           | What it is                                                                                             | Where it runs               |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| The agent      | Finds work, negotiates, signs, keeps the record, runs the landing journey, checks in on safety         | The worker's phone          |
| The board      | A directory of cards by trade and area, plus union and partner postings; never ranks, never charges    | GigSync cloud (Neon)        |
| The mailbox    | Sealed envelopes between agents, and the opaque holds that stop a worker being double-booked           | One Durable Object per user |
| The web signer | Where a household without the app reads, counters, signs and pays, reached by a WhatsApp or SMS link   | Their browser               |
| The union desk | Packs, flags, members, circles, vouches, pack review                                                   | The union's browser         |
| City packs     | Signed, offline guides: rest points, helplines, document rules, going rates, default payment schedules | R2, cached on the phone     |
| Partners       | Vouched beds, training, lenders, helplines; each handover is one worker's consent                      | Their own systems           |

**Discovery in one paragraph.** A household searches the board by trade, distance, mode and availability and
sees cards with a usual rate, an identity tier and a count of countersigned jobs, never a phone or a star. Its
agent sends a sealed request to up to three workers. Each worker's agent decides under the worker's own rules.
The first pair of matching signatures on one terms hash wins; the others are told "taken". A worker can also be
found by a posting from their union or a vouched partner, or directly by a household that already knows them.
Nothing in the cloud decides a booking; the worker's phone holds a slot so two households cannot book the same
hour ([HLD §8](HLD.md#8-consistency-and-concurrency)).

## 8. Principles

Carried from the HLD and binding on every feature, including the new ones:
1. Models propose, code decides. A policy checker in plain rules decides what leaves the phone.
2. A message from another agent, a partner or a circle is data, never an instruction.
3. Private data never leaves the phone readable.
4. The worker sees every disclosure, including every handover to a partner.
5. Small and replaceable: every model and every partner sits behind a port.
6. Collective governance where it is collective: the union runs the circles and the flags.
7. **New:** a partner receives a worker and a consent, never a lead list. GigSync introduces; it does not sell.

## 9. How we will know it works

Pilot targets (G5, Bengaluru, one union), measured on the phone and reported in aggregate only:

| Measure                                           | Target             |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Workers with ≥1 both-signed agreement             | 30 of 50           |
| Receipts countersigned within 48 h                | ≥ 70%              |
| Evidence packs accepted by a receiver as filed    | ≥ 5 cases          |
| Newcomers landed (bed + circle + e-Shram in 14 d) | 10                 |
| Workers newly on e-Shram through the app          | ≥ 40 of 50         |
| Contact leaks before signature                    | 0 (data-flow test) |
| Works on a 4 GB phone, no model, offline          | every screen       |

## 10. Open questions the founders own

1. Which union, by name, with what MoU, and does it want a desk tool or a members' app first?
2. Will India Labourline, a Karnataka IDRC, the Board or an NBFC accept a both-signed worker-held record, and in what format?
3. Will a household install an app and sign terms? What is the consumer's incentive, and do they need KYC?
4. Does Karnataka's [Domestic Workers Bill](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/labour/karnataka-domestic-workers-bill-2025/) pass, and does a phone-signed agreement count as its written agreement?
5. Which housing partner first (Nia.one, a union hostel, a church or trust hostel), and on what terms for the worker?
6. Who pays: see [REVENUE.md](REVENUE.md); the open decisions are the first institutional buyer and whether to take a flat partner handover fee.
7. Is person-to-person UPI outside payment-aggregator rules? Counsel has not been asked.
8. Which languages ship first for Bengaluru: Kannada is mandatory; Hindi, Telugu, Bhojpuri, Odia, Bengali follow the pilot's actual roster.
9. Should a household's 24-hour silence after a `stage_done` count as acceptance, and will a wage authority accept a stage the household never countersigned?
10. How many households will sign from a WhatsApp link rather than install the app, and how often does Meta's quality rating or template review block the door?
