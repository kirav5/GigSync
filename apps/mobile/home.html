The idea ↗︎

01 / Rooted in work

Honest work, with
respect for both sides.

Connecting homes that need help with people who do it best.

Direct. Private. Built for everyday work.

One connected circle.

Inspired by Warli art, with gratitude to the Warli community.

In traditional Warli paintings, life is drawn as a circle: village, work, and community held together without a hierarchy.

That is the spirit behind GigSync: technology designed to stay quietly in the background while real people connect on their own terms.

02 / Built especially for low-end phones

  1. Be found

    Found by the homes nearby.

    Homes near you find you on the network, or scan the QR on your card. Either way, they ask your assistant directly.

    Your card shows your trade, your area and your languages. Your number stays hidden.

  2. Clarity before commitment

    Everything clear, before you say yes.

    Work, hours, place and pay arrive in one request, before you say a word.

    No surprise tasks, no unclear hours. If something is missing, your assistant asks.

  3. Negotiation within your limits

    Your limits, kept private.

    Your assistant bargains only inside the limits you set: your rate, your days off, how far you will go.

    Your lowest rate never leaves your phone. The household sees only the offer.

  4. Never a deal without approval

    Nothing happens without your OK.

    Every offer waits for you. Say okay, ask for a change, or say no.

    Your assistant suggests; you decide. It never agrees on your behalf.

  5. Agree fair terms

    The contract, written for you.

    The hardest part, done for you: pay, hours and days off in plain words, signed on both phones.

    Both phones keep the same signed copy, so nobody can change it later.

  6. Keep what you earn

    Paid in full, by UPI.

    The household pays straight to your UPI. No cut taken.

    A receipt signed by both phones, not a screenshot.

  7. Your data, strictly yours

    Your number, only until it's paid.

    Phone numbers are shared after signing and hidden again once the job is paid.

    Your chats and your limits stay on your phone.

  8. Your trust travels with you

    A record that's yours to keep.

    Every job and payment goes into an open record you own. Read it, share it, take it anywhere.

    No app can take your work history away.

  9. A familiar circle

    New city, familiar faces.

    A mentor who knows the work and the place, and the going rate before your first job.

    Help nearby, in your language.
