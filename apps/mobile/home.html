How a job works

Agree first. Then share.

The worker's agent and the customer's agent settle the terms under rules the worker sets. The worker's contact goes out only after both sign.

Privacy wall

You hold the record. You set the rules.

The platform, the contractor or the agency used to keep your work history. Now it is on your phone.

On your phone

Your data stays with you

Jobs, pay, rules and notes live on your phone. The cloud only passes locked messages.

Plain rules

Your rules decide, not a model

What is shared, how precisely and for how long is set by rules you choose. AI may draft; it never decides.

Every disclosure

You see what was shared

"What my agent shared" lists each fact sent, to whom and under which rule. You can revoke any rule.

Union governed

Shared flags, decided together

Members decide what a "do not accept" flag means and who may raise one. Planned with the pilot union.

When something goes wrong

The proof is already yours.

Wages held back, an ID blocked, harassment at work: you pick who sees which part. Nothing leaves your phone without your tap.

Built small

For a ₹10,000 phone. Works with no model at all.

One codebase for Android, iPhone and the web. Agreements, records and rules are plain code; a small model only helps with words.

No model needed

Rules and templates first

Every core step works with no model: agreeing, holding back contact, keeping the record, getting paid.

Offline model

On the device, never the cloud

LiteRT-LM on Android, Apple's on-device model on iPhone, an in-browser engine on the web. No frontier API.
