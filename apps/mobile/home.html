Inspired by Warli art, with gratitude to the Warli community.

In traditional Warli paintings, life is drawn as a circle—village, work, and community held together without a hierarchy.

That is the spirit behind GigSync: technology designed to stay quietly in the background while real people connect on their own terms.

02 / Privacy by design

Your agent, on your phone.
Your data, strictly yours.

Powered by a local AI model running directly on your smartphone. Your conversations, work history, and intent stay strictly on your device—never uploaded to external servers, never mined, and never shared without your say.

On-device AI · Zero data leaves your phone

01

Smart replies

Less back-and-forth before work.

Your on-device assistant answers routine questions about your availability, rates, and area—filtering out bad fits so you only talk to serious matches.

Language tools provide profound opportunity to explore and deeply understand our peers. Agents not only answer redundant questions but enable each one of us to collaborate by understanding each one's preferences, priorities, and boundaries.

In practice

A worker goes home to Bihar for the harvest. Their assistant finds work that respects that time with family.

02

Portable work record

Your track record stays yours.

Every completed job and verified receipt is saved directly on your phone—not locked inside one app—so you can prove your experience anywhere you go.

During our conversations with gig workers, one theme always came up. ID blocks on a platform might seem insignificant to some, but for a gig worker it means migrating to a new platform, with no leverage or credentials to distinguish themselves based on their prior work.

The idea

A record of your work and the trust you have earned, carried with you and shared on your terms.

03

Contract writing

Clarity before commitment.

Rate, hours, weekly off, leave and the payment schedule, agreed and signed by both phones before any work begins.

We believe price encapsulates expectation around quality of service.

Expectation being subjective, any divergence in perception around quality of work between a customer and a gig worker may lead to a small dispute, as is natural. So the contract must also include pre-emptive measures to reach mutual understanding in case any issues occur.

Preventing small disputes from turning into serious conflicts.

Agree in advance

What is included, what good work means, and how both people will resolve a concern.

06 / Built for new arrivals

New city.
Fair pay from day one.

Know the going rate before you accept your first job—even when you’re brand new in town.

Workers have told us about moving to a city because a brother, a friend, or someone from their village was already there. A familiar person can make an unfamiliar world feel safer. Most of the women we spoke to found their first work through someone, not through an app.

GigSync helps bridge the gap. It acts as a guide, ready to help workers get a fair understanding of the price of their work in a new city. It shows the usual pay for their work there, so they never have to guess or settle for less. When a household asks, their assistant agrees the price, the hours and the day off within the worker’s own rules.

07 / Built around real life

Familiar ways in.
Personal boundaries intact.

Conversations with workers are shaping the product. These are the principles guiding what we build next.

01Next interfaceMeet people where they already are.WhatsApp

A household signs the terms and pays from a WhatsApp or SMS link without installing anything. The link carries a notice and nothing else; the terms, the contact cards and the record never pass through Meta's servers.

02Access by designIntelligence within reach.Local models

Today our model supports English and Hinglish. We are striving to build an inclusive community, so models supporting other regional languages locally on the phone come as the next step.

03Privacy by defaultShare what is relevant. Keep the rest hers.Her permission

Private context stays on her device. Messages between assistants travel sealed through a mailbox the server cannot read.

"Are you free on Tuesday?"

Her assistant shares an approved time window—not her full calendar, her home address, or her number.

Each interaction has a defined scope. Her number and address move only after both phones sign, and only until the job ends.
