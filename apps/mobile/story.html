04 / In practice

Two phones.
One agreement.

Lakshmi cleans homes in Koramangala. Priya's family needs help on weekday mornings. Scroll, and one phone at a time shows what each of them sees: from her first hello to the first payment, and the record that stays with her.

Lakshmi's phonePriya's phoneOne phone at a time. Illustrative screens; no live agent, quote or agreement.

01Her first minute · Monday, 7:40 am

A hello, in her language.

No tour. No form. Her agent asks one thing at a time, and says why it asks.

Kannada, Hindi or English. Voice first, because typing is slow.

02A name and a trade

Nice to meet you, Lakshmi.

Her name, her trade, the areas she works. Nothing she has not said herself.

Every answer can be changed later. Nothing is final on the first screen.

03Her rules

Her rules, in her words.

By the visit or by the month. The days she keeps, how far she will go, the hours she will not work. She says a rule, and it is kept.

The going rate nearby is shown, never imposed. There is no fixed wage here, and no rule that the agent or a household can override.

04Her number

Her number stays on her phone.

Before anything is asked of her, the agent says what it will do. A household sees her number only after both sign.

No permission prompt without a reason first.

05Ready · Monday, 7:46 am

Six minutes. Her agent is ready.

Everything she said is on her phone and nowhere else. From now on her agent listens for work that fits her rules, and wakes her only then.

No account to create, no document to upload, nothing sent anywhere.

06Across town · Tuesday, 9:02 am

Priya needs help on weekday mornings.

She types it the way she would say it. Her agent turns it into a sealed request and asks nearby workers' agents, with no name or number attached.

Priya can do this from a WhatsApp link too, without installing anything.

07A fit, under her rules

Checked before she is asked.

Trade, distance, hours and every rule she set. Lakshmi hears about a job only when it fits, and one tap lets her agent answer.

The check runs on her phone. Nothing in the cloud decides this.

08The agents talk

₹450, Sunday off.
She only confirms.

Her agent and Priya's agent talk in plain turns she can read. Structured offers, capped counters, never below her rules. She can step in at any turn.

Two turns here. Four at most, so a household's agent cannot wear hers down.

09Both sign

The same chat, on both phones.

Priya sees the same turns, word for word, and signs on her own phone. Only now does Lakshmi's number cross, and only for this job.

Her address is never sent. Her phone holds the slot, so no one else can book the same hours.

10Paid the same day · Friday, 11:05 am

A receipt a visit.
UPI, straight to her.

Priya countersigns each visit on her own phone. The money goes to Lakshmi's UPI, not through anyone.

GigSync never holds a rupee, never routes a payment, never takes a cut.

11What stays hers · three months on

Twenty-three jobs.
No stars.

The record lives on her phone, signed by both sides. She chooses who sees which part of it.

"Worked together, 23 times" is a fact, with its source. A rating is not.

12A new city · next year

A familiar circle, wherever she goes.

The record comes with her. So do the going rates, a vouched bed, her union's circle, and the registrations she is owed.

A partner receives a person and a consent, never a list.
