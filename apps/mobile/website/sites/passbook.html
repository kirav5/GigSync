Your work. Your record. On your phone.

GigSync gives a domestic or home-service worker an agent that agrees each job, keeps the signed proof of every visit and payment, and lands them in a new city with a bed, a circle and the welfare they are owed. It never takes a cut, ranks a worker, or holds the money.

SmallWorks with no model at all

Every screen runs on a 4 GB phone, offline. A small open model on the device only helps with wording. Nothing in GigSync calls a cloud model.

IntentionalThe worker sees every disclosure

Private data never leaves the phone readable. The union, a helpline or a hostel sees only what the worker taps to send.

TailoredBengaluru, one union, Kannada first

Designed with domestic workers and their union. Households sign from a WhatsApp or SMS link without installing anything.

How a job works

Agree first. Then share.

The first pair of matching signatures on one terms hash wins. Until then, her number and address stay on her phone. After the job ends, they are withdrawn again.

Household asksThe job, time window, budget. No name, no number.
Her agent matchesSkill, distance, hours, her minimum rate.
Agents negotiateStructured offers, capped counters. She can step in.
Both phones signRate, time, scope. Contact released only now.
Job done, UPI paidHousehold pays her directly. Both ledgers update.
Built small

For a ₹10,000 phone. No frontier model anywhere.

Matching, negotiation and the ledger are plain rules. One small open model, under 3B parameters, 4-bit, only helps with wording and speech. The app is complete with no model at all.
