Human-scale AI · a worker's own agent · Bengaluru

Her number stays on her phone until both sides sign.

This is one week with GigSync, told from Lakshmi's phone. Scroll, and the phone on the right shows what she sees. The agent speaks Kannada, works with no internet and no model, and never takes a cut.

Monday · 7:40 am

She asks for work in her own words.

Lakshmi holds the button and says, in Kannada, that she is free Monday to Friday mornings in Koramangala. The small model on her phone names the intent and fills the slots: trade, area, days. Code checks the reply against a schema and computes every number. If there is no model, keyword lists do the same.

  • Speech on device first; cloud speech only with consent (ADR 0023)
  • One on-phone model router, JSON checked by a schema (ADR 0040)
  • Chat text never leaves the phone for routing
Monday · 9:15 am

Her agent negotiates. She only confirms.

A household's agent sends a sealed request. Lakshmi's agent answers under her rules: at least ₹400 a visit, Sunday off, four days' leave. The two agents settle on ₹450, paid by the day. Nothing private crosses the wall; her phone and address are held.

  • Rate, hours, weekly off, leave and a payment schedule in the terms
  • Unpredictable negotiation tactics so a household's agent cannot game hers (ADR 0033)
  • Request to at most three workers; first pair of signatures wins
Monday · 9:22 am

Both phones sign the same terms. Then, and only then, her number is released.

Two signatures on one terms hash. The household gets her number for the length of the job; her address is never sent. Her phone holds the slot so no second household can book the same hours.

  • Ed25519 signatures on the device; terms hash shared, terms sealed
  • Contact leaks before signature: the pilot target is zero, and a data-flow test enforces it
  • Household without the app signs from a WhatsApp or SMS link
Friday · 11:05 am

Each visit is a receipt. Each receipt is paid by UPI, straight to her.

After the visit, her agent sends a receipt; the household countersigns within 48 hours and pays by UPI to the one UPI ID in her profile. GigSync never holds a rupee, never routes the payment, never takes a cut.

  • Payment by day or by stage, countersigned receipts, no escrow (ADR 0022)
  • Payment auto-detected from the UPI notification (ADR 0012)
  • Earnings and job record kept per owner on the phone, with a server-held copy for a lost phone (ADR 0037)
Three months later

She moves to Hyderabad. The record moves with her.

The agent walks her from "I am going" to settled: real going rates from countersigned records, a vouched partner bed, a circle of workers from her region run by her union, e-Shram and state-board registration, the documents she needs, a cash plan for the first two weeks.

  • City packs: signed, offline guides on the phone
  • Partners receive a worker and a consent, never a lead list
  • Registration helper and eligibility counter for welfare she is owed (ADR 0019)

Human-scale AI

Small. Intentional. Tailored.

What made each chapter above possible, and the decision behind it.

SmallNo cloud model, by design

LiteRT-LM on Android, Apple Foundation Models on iPhone, the browser's own model on the web. The model names an intent; code decides. Lite mode with no model is complete.

IntentionalThe worker sees every disclosure

Private data never leaves the phone readable. "What my agent shared" lists every envelope. The union runs the circles and flags, not GigSync.

TailoredDomestic workers, Bengaluru, Kannada

Voice first for low-literacy use. Households sign from a link. The landing journey exists because workers move cities and start from zero.

What GigSync is not

Nothing we earn can depend on her earning less.

No cutNot a percentage of any job, not an access fee, not paid placement.
No rankingNo stars. "Worked together, countersigned, N times" is a fact, not a score.
No money heldUPI goes to the worker. No wallet, no escrow, no lending.
No worker databaseKeys, cards, sealed envelopes and a partner registry. Nothing readable.
