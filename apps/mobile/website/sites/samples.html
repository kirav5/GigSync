Server-side profiles. Her data lives encrypted on her phone; she can leave with her record.

Private by constructionSkills, rates, history, location in an encrypted store on the phone. The relay sees who, whom and when. Never what.
Public by choiceOnly a signed record she publishes: what she offers and a coarse area. She edits or withdraws any time.
Her rules, not a modelMinimum rate, hours, areas, flagged customers are plain rules the agent cannot override. Consent per purpose, in her language, under DPDP.
ForkableIdentity, records and protocol are open. A workers' collective can run its own relay or fork the app.
Tailored₹10k

Built for a 4–6 GB Android on patchy 4G, in Hindi, Hinglish and English, from what workers told us.

Status

Where we are, honestly.

Built

Encrypted store, device keys, locked mailboxX25519 sealing, Ed25519 signatures; one mailbox per user at the edge; open design.
App shell and sign-inAndroid, iPhone, Mac and web from one codebase. Lite mode with no model.
Policy rules with shared test casesPlain rules decide what leaves the phone; the same cases run on every platform.
Indian ID sanitizerAadhaar, PAN and phone patterns stripped in code before any model sees text.

In progress

Job matching for home servicesBy trade, distance, time window and her minimum rate.
Agent-to-agent negotiationStructured offers, capped counters, she can step in at any turn.
Choosing and benchmarking the on-device modelSub-3B candidates, 4-bit, tokens per second on a ₹10–15k phone.
Removing every cloud-inference pathSo "does core functionality call a frontier API" is answered no, provably.
