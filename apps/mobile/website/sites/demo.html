An agent that fits in a ₹10,000 phone.

GigSync gives a gig worker their own agent, on their own phone, for their whole working life. It finds work, agrees each job with the household's agent, holds back the worker's number until both sides sign, and keeps a signed record of every job and payment that belongs to the worker. No cloud model. No cut. No ranking.

What we are building

One agent per worker. Nothing in the middle.

A job is two agents and two signatures. The cloud carries sealed envelopes it cannot read, a directory of cards by trade and area, and a partner registry. It never decides a booking, ranks a worker, or holds a rupee.

On the phoneThe agent

Finds work in any mode (one-off, part-time, full-time placement, platform shifts), negotiates under the worker's own rules, signs, keeps the hash-chained record, runs the landing journey, checks in on safety.

  • Device keys; X25519 sealing; Ed25519 signatures
  • Policy checker in plain rules decides what leaves
  • Works with no model installed

In the cloudThe board and the mailbox

A directory of cards by trade, distance and availability, never ranked, never charged. One mailbox per user holds sealed envelopes between agents and the opaque holds that stop a worker being double-booked.

  • Cloudflare Workers, one Durable Object per user
  • Neon Postgres directory
  • Public: a card, a usual rate, a count of countersigned jobs. Never a phone or a star

In a browserThe web signer and the union desk

A household without the app reads, counters, signs and pays from a WhatsApp or SMS link. A union steward runs circles, issues flags members trust, and receives only what a member chooses to send.

  • WhatsApp is a door, never the agent (ADR 0021)
  • Union desk: packs, flags, members, circles, vouches
  • City packs: signed, offline guides on R2
How a job works

Agree first. Then share.

The first pair of matching signatures on one terms hash wins. Until then, the worker's number and address stay on their phone. After the job ends, they are withdrawn again.

RequestHousehold's agent seals a request to up to three workers by trade and area.
RulesEach worker's agent answers under the worker's own rules: minimum rate, days off, leave, notice.
TermsRate, hours, weekly off, leave and a payment schedule: by the day, by the stage, or by the month.
Both signTwo signatures on one terms hash. Contact released, slot held on the phone.
WorkEach visit or stage is a receipt the household countersigns within 48 hours.
PaidUPI straight to the worker at each stage. GigSync never holds the money.
RecordEvery agreement, visit and payment, hash-chained on the phone, exportable by the worker only.
Human-scale AI

Small. Intentional. Tailored.

Mozilla's three principles, each with the decision that makes it true in GigSync and the ADR that records it.

SmallModels propose, code decides

Nothing in GigSync calls a cloud model. The only models are small, open ones on the phone, and the app is complete with none installed ("lite mode").

  • On-device engines: LiteRT-LM on Android, Apple Foundation Models on iPhone and Mac, the browser's built-in model on the web
  • The model only names an intent and fills slots; its reply is JSON checked by a schema; code computes every number (ADR 0040)
  • Keyword router answers when there is no model, when it is slow, or when its reply fails the schema
  • Speech on device first; cloud speech only with consent (ADR 0023)

IntentionalThe worker holds the record

Private data never leaves the phone readable. Every disclosure, including every handover to a partner, is shown to the worker before it happens.

  • Signed, hash-chained record on the device; exports only the part the worker picks
  • Data kept per owner on the phone (ADR 0032); chat text never leaves for routing
  • "What my agent shared": a screen listing every envelope sent
  • Union runs the circles and the flags; GigSync does not own the community (ADR 0017)

TailoredBuilt with one union, in Kannada

Domestic, beauty and home-service workers in Bengaluru first, with their union. Delivery, rides and construction on the same core later.

  • Kannada mandatory; Hindi, Telugu, Bhojpuri, Odia, Bengali follow the pilot roster
  • Voice first: a worker speaks, the agent writes the terms
  • Households sign from a WhatsApp or SMS link, no install
  • Landing journey for workers arriving in a new city: bed, circle, e-Shram, documents

What GigSync is not

Eight refusals, on purpose.

Nothing GigSync earns may depend on the worker earning less. Each refusal is a design decision with an ADR behind it.

Not a platformNo employer, agency or platform in the middle; a job is two agents and two signatures.
Not a bankUPI goes straight to the worker. No wallet, no escrow, no lending.
Not a rating systemNo stars. A record says "worked together, countersigned, N times", which is a fact.
Not a database of workersThe cloud holds keys, public cards, locked envelopes and a partner registry.
Not AI-dependentWorks with no model at all; a small offline model only helps with speech and wording.
Not a WhatsApp botWhatsApp and SMS carry a notice and a link. The agent, the terms and the record never pass through Meta's servers.
Not a landlord or a lenderPartners are. GigSync hands over a worker and a consent, never a lead list.
Not taking a cutNo percentage of any job, no access fee, no paid placement, no selling of data or footage.

Questions

Asked by reviewers, unions and households.

Does it work without AI?

Yes. Every screen works with no model installed, on a 4 GB phone, offline. A small open model on the device only helps with speech and wording, and its output is checked by code before anything happens.

What does the cloud see?

Keys, public cards, sealed envelopes it cannot open, opaque holds, and a partner registry. Never a phone number, an address, a chat, or the record.

How does GigSync make money without a cut?

Institutions pay for outcomes: a registered, day-counted worker and a filed, evidenced grievance, the way welfare boards and CSR programmes already pay. No line of revenue rises when a worker earns less.

Will a household install an app?

They do not have to. A WhatsApp or SMS link opens the web signer, where they read, counter, sign and pay. The pilot measures how many sign from a link rather than install.

Why a union and not a community feature?

Every global peer-community tool for gig workers failed on community-building, not code. Unions already hold the trust; GigSync gives them tooling and governance and does not try to be the community.
