A worker-held agent for India's gig economy

The agent is on the phone. The cloud only carries sealed envelopes.

GigSync is a gig worker's agent for their whole working life: it finds work in any mode, agrees each job with the household's agent, and keeps a signed, hash-chained record on the device. No central database of workers, no cloud model, no cut. Pilot: 50 domestic workers, one union, Bengaluru.

How a job works

Agree first. Then share.

The first pair of matching signatures on one terms hash wins. Until then, her number and address stay on her phone. After the job ends, they are withdrawn again.

Household asksThe job, time window, budget. No name, no number.
Her agent matchesSkill, distance, hours, her minimum rate.
Agents negotiateStructured offers, capped counters. She can step in.
Both phones signRate, time, scope. Contact released only now.
Job done, UPI paidHousehold pays her directly. Both ledgers update.
Built small

For a ₹10,000 phone. No frontier model anywhere.

Matching, negotiation and the ledger are plain rules. One small open model, under 3B parameters, 4-bit, only helps with wording and speech. The app is complete with no model at all.
