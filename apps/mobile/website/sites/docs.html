GigSync · a worker-held agent · proposal to Mozilla Human-Scale AI

The agent is on the phone. The cloud only carries sealed envelopes.

GigSync is a gig worker's own agent for their whole working life. It finds work in any mode, agrees each job with the household's agent, and keeps a signed, hash-chained record on the device. No central database of workers, no cloud model, no cut. Pilot: 50 domestic workers, one union, Bengaluru.

Working code on maine2e on real devicesZero cloud model callsPilot: Aug–Oct 2027

02The problem

India's platforms keep the record and take the cut. Government registries hold what platforms upload, not what the worker did off-platform. Workers who move cities start from zero, pay fake "job agents" ₹4,800 to register, and sleep in ₹500 windowless rooms because nothing sits between those and ₹8,000 PGs.

Urban Company core~₹100 on a ₹767 job (worker accounts)28% commission, ₹1,000 ten-day plans, credit floats, kits, ID blocks

03What we built

A job is two agents and two signatures. Everything else is a port behind the agent: a mailbox that cannot read, a board that does not rank, a web signer for a household with no app, a desk for the union, a registry of vouched partners.

PhoneThe agent

  • Negotiates under the worker's rules
  • Signs; holds the slot
  • Hash-chained record
  • Landing journey, offline

CloudBoard and mailbox

  • Cards by trade and area
  • Sealed envelopes, opaque holds
  • Workers + one Durable Object per user
  • Neon Postgres directory

BrowserSigner and desk

  • WhatsApp or SMS link to sign and pay
  • Union: circles, flags, members, vouches
  • City packs on R2

04How a job works

The first pair of matching signatures on one terms hash wins; the others are told "taken". Nothing in the cloud decides a booking.

RequestSealed, to at most three workers
RulesMin rate, days off, leave, notice
TermsRate, hours, off, leave, schedule
Both signOne hash, two signatures; contact released
WorkReceipt per visit or stage, countersigned in 48 h
PaidUPI direct, no escrow
RecordHash-chained on the phone

05Small

Nothing in GigSync calls a cloud model. The only models are small, open ones on the phone, and the app is complete with none installed.

Engines
LiteRT-LM on Android; Apple Foundation Models on iPhone and Mac; the browser's built-in model on the web; Ollama in development builds only
Largest model
Under 3B parameters, 4-bit, optional. The form's answer: "10B or fewer".
What the model does
Names an intent and fills slots from a fixed list (trade, area, city, amount, contact). Reply is JSON checked by a zod schema; slot values must be ones code already knows. Code runs the action and computes every number. (ADR 0040)
Fallback
Keyword lists in English, Hindi, Kannada and Hinglish answer when there is no model, when it is slow, or when its reply fails the schema. Measured by an eval set, reported per intent in the build.
Speech
On device first (IndicConformer under test); cloud speech only with explicit consent (ADR 0023)
Hardware floor
Every screen on a 4 GB phone, offline. Lite mode is a test target, not a degraded mode.

06Intentional

Private data never leaves the phone readable. The worker sees every disclosure, including every handover to a partner, before it happens.

Record
Signed, hash-chained, on the device; exported only in the part the worker picks, to whoever they pick
Data per owner
One record per owner on the phone; another owner on the same phone sees none of it (ADR 0032)
Disclosure
"What my agent shared" lists every envelope sent. A partner receives a worker and a consent, never a lead list.
Governance
The union runs the circles and issues the flags members trust (ADR 0017). GigSync does not own the community.
Lost phone
A server-held copy of the job record, sealed to the worker's keys, so earnings survive a moved phone (ADR 0037)

07Tailored

Built with domestic workers and their union in Bengaluru. The people, the problem and the city shaped what it does, how it works and how it is deployed.

Language
Kannada mandatory; Hindi, Telugu, Bhojpuri, Odia, Bengali follow the pilot roster. Titles never cut through a Kannada or Hindi syllable.
Voice first
A worker speaks; the agent writes the terms. For low-literacy use and a phone held in one hand on a bus.
The household
Signs from a WhatsApp or SMS link without installing anything (ADR 0021). The pilot measures how many do.
Moving
The landing journey exists because workers move cities and start from zero: bed, circle, e-Shram, documents, a cash plan for the first two weeks
Payment
UPI, one ID in the profile, auto-detected from the notification; schedules by day, stage or month because that is how these jobs are paid (ADR 0022)

13Sustainability

No platform that refuses a cut has published a profit. The only near-sustainable models without a cut are paid per outcome by an institution. GigSync sells exactly that.

Sold
A registered, day-counted worker and a filed, evidenced grievance: the outputs of the Record and Landing pillars
Buyers
Karnataka's gig-worker welfare board; CSR programmes in the Haqdarshak mould; the state domestic-workers board once the Bill passes; foundations already funding Bengaluru gig-worker infrastructure
Test
No line of revenue rises when a worker's rate falls, waits longer, is steered, or is ranked
Open questions
First institutional buyer; whether to take a flat partner handover fee; whether person-to-person UPI sits outside payment-aggregator rules (counsel not yet asked)
