01 / The circle

More than a worker.
A person, with people.

For the woman who cleans three homes before noon. The cook who arrived with one phone number. The steward who answers the union's WhatsApp group at night. Work in India has always moved through people who vouch for each other. GigSync keeps it that way.

02 / Her agent

She says it once.
Her agent does the asking.

"Can you do it? By when? At what price?" Her agent begins the conversation, in Kannada or Hindi, within rules she set: her minimum rate, her Sunday off, the areas she will travel to. A proposal comes back for her tap. Nothing private leaves her phone.

Why a conversation matters: the questions are always the same, and they arrive while she is working.

03 / Her record

She earned the trust.
It should stay hers.

A platform account should not be the only home for her working life. Every agreement, visit and payment is signed on her phone and chained together, so it cannot be edited behind her back or deleted when she leaves. When she moves to another city, it moves with her.

What should travel with her? Everything she chooses, and nothing she doesn't.

04 / A new city

New city.
A familiar circle.

Sometimes a new beginning starts with one person you know. Workers told us they moved because a brother, a friend or someone from the village was already there. When there is no one, the agent walks her from "I am going" to settled.

  1. 01Before she leavesReal going rates for her trade, which union is there, what the first two weeks cost. A warning about anyone charging a "fee to register".
  2. 02TonightA verified bed from a vouched partner near where she will work. The receipt lands in her record.
  3. 03This weekHer circle: people from her region and trade, run by the union. A mentor opts in.
  4. 04This monthe-Shram and the state board, SIM, bank account, Aadhaar address. The welfare she is owed, counted.
05 / Promises to the circle

What we will never do.

Nothing GigSync earns may depend on a worker earning less. Each line is a decision written down in the open.

No cut.Not a percentage of any job, not an access fee, not paid placement.
No ranking.No stars. Countersigned work is the only record.
No money held.UPI goes from the household to her. No wallet, no escrow.
No database of workers.The cloud holds locked envelopes it cannot read.
No cloud model.One small open model on her phone, and the app works with none.
No lead lists.A partner receives one worker and one consent, never a list.
